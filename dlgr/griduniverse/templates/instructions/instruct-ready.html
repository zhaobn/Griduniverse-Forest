Objective

Your goal is to maximize points within 30 actions by collecting, combining, and consuming items in a grid world. Explore the world to discover new, more rewarding items and maximize your score.


How to play:

  1. Move Your Avatar: Use the arrow keys to navigate the grid world.
  2. Pick Up an Item: Stand on an item and press the spacebar to pick it up.
  3. Drop an Item: Press the D key to drop the item you are holding.
  4. Consume an Item (1 action): While holding an item, press the spacebar to consume it and earn its points.
  5. Combine Items (1 action): While holding an item, stand on top of another item and press the spacebar. If the items can combine, they will transform into a new item that is 10 times more rewarding.

Item points:

  • Base Items (Red): These are the starting items, worth 1 point each.
  • Combining Items: When you combine two items successfully, you create a new item that is 10 times more rewarding! For example, two red items can create an orange item, worth 10 points.
  • Items are ranked by level, shown as rainbow colors: Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Purple
  • The level of a new item is one level higher than the highest-level item used in its combination.

Strategy Tip:

Use your 30 actions wisely: Discovering and creating higher-level items is key to earning more points—focus on finding and combining items to unlock new, more valuable levels!

To help you keep track of combinations and item points, throughout the game, you will see the history of combinations you've tried, and the points value of items you've consumed or discovered

After the game

At the end of the game, you'll have the chance to leave a message for a future player. Share any knowledge or tips you think will help them achieve a higher score.

You will receive a bonus based on how many points you collect, and how well the future player who reads your message performs.
